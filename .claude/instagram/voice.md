## Notes for the skills: Arabic

Tested 2026-10-06 on Gulf Arabic mixed with English terms. Nothing crashed, but
word counts and keyword lists are Latin-only, so Arabic words vanish. Run tools
as `python3 -I -B`, as tested (without -B swipe.py writes `__pycache__` into the pack). In a READY
receipt, write "checked by hand" for any check marked ignore; never its number.

- **hookscore.py:** ignore scores, ranking, verdict and the "under 50, no hook"
  rule: a pure Arabic hook always gets LENGTH 0 and FRONTLOAD 0 (four hooks
  scored 32/32/20/8 in Arabic, 87/81/52/8 in English). The hashtag and emoji
  dealbreakers work; the greeting check does not, so reject by eye a hook that
  opens with السلام عليكم، هلا، مرحبا، اليوم بتكلم عن، في هذا الفيديو. Rank by
  hand against `hooks.json`. ASCII 30 counts fully; ٣٠ counts as a number but
  not as the opening payload.
- **beats.py:** ignore its timings, labels and "add N words"; `--wpm` cannot fix
  it (a 62-word script read as 5 words, 1.8 s). Time by hand, one beat per line,
  his wpm in place of 150 (on the test script it gave the same per-line seconds
  and total as beats.py on a Latin stand-in: 62 words, 24.8 s):
  `awk -v wpm=150 '{s=NF*60/wpm; printf "%5.1fs %4.1fs  %s\n", t, s, $0; t+=s} END{printf "total %.1fs\n", t}' script.txt`
  Then check by hand: hook line 3 s or less (about 7 words at 150), no beat
  over 4 s, a number or name every few beats, last line echoes a hook word.
- **caption.py:** trust LENGTH, FIRST LINE, LINKS, EMOJI, SEARCH TERMS (plain
  substring: exact spelling, ال included) and the text in the 125-character box
  (it counts characters). Read the text, not the borders: shadda and emoji shift
  them. Ignore HASHTAGS and TAG PLACEMENT (Arabic tags are invisible: 7 read as
  3, PASS), ONE ASK (blind to Arabic asks, even "علّق بكلمة PROMPT"), and names in
  HOOK IS CONCRETE (sees digits, not ChatGPT or Arabic names). By hand: one ask,
  no # in the box, `grep -o '#[^[:space:]]*' caption.txt | wc -l` 5 or less.
- **humanize.py:** do not run it on Arabic. It deletes RLM, LRM and Arabic
  letter marks (they keep mixed lines in order) plus ZWNJ/ZWJ, `--lexicon`
  cannot stop it, and it turns em dashes into ASCII ", " (leaving "،,"). It has
  no Arabic slop list. Do em dash to ، and the never-say list by hand. For
  English slop hits only (report is on stderr): `python3 -I -B humanize.py draft.txt --report > /dev/null`.
- **detect.py:** ignore HUMAN SCORE and verdict; skip "repeat until PASS".
  Arabic cannot PASS (SPECIFICITY and VOICE stay at 50); a natural Gulf post came
  out FLAGGED, and FINGERPRINT punishes direction marks. Only BURSTINESS told
  human (0.31) from formulaic (0.15) Arabic: a hint, not a gate. Instead read it
  aloud and run the never-say list; receipt: "humanizer: Arabic, checked by hand".
- **swipe.py:** trust the outlier ranking (it parses Arabic-Indic digits like
  ٤١٢٬٠٠٠). Ignore formula names (Arabic hooks come back "unclassified"), hook
  scores, hook word counts and the median hook lines.
- **Greetings:** "أهلين" opens his intro reel; "سلام" (hand graphic) then "وشحالكم؟" (two frames,
  no comma) close its loop: a greeting, "how are you all?", not a goodbye (inferred). Never
  end a regular reel on وشحالكم؟ (default). A hook line never opens with a greeting, his included.
